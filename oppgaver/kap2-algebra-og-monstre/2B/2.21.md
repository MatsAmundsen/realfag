Bruk kvadratsetningene:

**a)** $(x + 7)^2$

**b)** $(y - 3)^2$

**c)** $(5 + a)(5 - a)$

## Hint

$(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$, $(a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$, $(a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2$.

## Fasit

**a)**
$(x + 7)^2 = x^2 + 2 \cdot x \cdot 7 + 7^2 = x^2 + 14x + 49$

**Svar:** $x^2 + 14x + 49$

[STEG]

**b)**
$(y - 3)^2 = y^2 - 2 \cdot y \cdot 3 + 9 = y^2 - 6y + 9$

**Svar:** $y^2 - 6y + 9$

[STEG]

**c)**
$(5 + a)(5 - a) = 5^2 - a^2 = 25 - a^2$

**Svar:** $25 - a^2$
