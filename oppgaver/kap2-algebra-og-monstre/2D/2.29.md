Faktoriser:

**a)** $x^2 - 49$

**b)** $x^2 + 6x + 9$

**c)** $4x^2 - 12x + 9$

## Hint

Differanse av to kvadrat: $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$. Fullstendig kvadrat: $a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2 = (a \pm b)^2$.

## Fasit

**a)**
$x^2 - 49 = (x - 7)(x + 7)$

**Svar:** $(x - 7)(x + 7)$

[STEG]

**b)**
$x^2 + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)^2$

**Svar:** $(x + 3)^2$

[STEG]

**c)**
$4x^2 - 12x + 9 = (2x - 3)^2$

**Svar:** $(2x - 3)^2$
