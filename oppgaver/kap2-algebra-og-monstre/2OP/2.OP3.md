Regn ut:

**a)** $(x + 4)^2$

**b)** $(3 - a)^2$

**c)** $(2b + 5)(2b - 5)$

## Hint

$(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$, $(a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$, $(a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2$.

## Fasit

**a)**
$(x + 4)^2 = x^2 + 2 \cdot x \cdot 4 + 4^2 = x^2 + 8x + 16$

**Svar:** $x^2 + 8x + 16$

[STEG]

**b)**
$(3 - a)^2 = 3^2 - 2 \cdot 3 \cdot a + a^2 = 9 - 6a + a^2$

**Svar:** $a^2 - 6a + 9$ (eller $9 - 6a + a^2$)

[STEG]

**c)**
$(2b + 5)(2b - 5) = (2b)^2 - 5^2 = 4b^2 - 25$

**Svar:** $4b^2 - 25$
