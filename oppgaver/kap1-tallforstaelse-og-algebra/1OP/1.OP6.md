Forenkle:

**a)** $a^5 \cdot a^{-2}$

**b)** $\dfrac{(2x)^3}{2x^{-1}}$

**c)** $(y^{-2})^3 \cdot y^5$


## Fasit

**a)**
$a^5 \cdot a^{-2} = a^{5 + (-2)} = a^3$

**Svar:** $a^3$

[STEG]

**b)**
$(2x)^3 = 2^3 \cdot x^3 = 8x^3$

Nevner: $2x^{-1} = 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{2}{x}$

$\dfrac{8x^3}{\frac{2}{x}} = 8x^3 \cdot \dfrac{x}{2} = 4x^4$

**Svar:** $4x^4$

[STEG]

**c)**
$(y^{-2})^3 = y^{-6}$

$y^{-6} \cdot y^5 = y^{-6 + 5} = y^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{y}$

**Svar:** $\dfrac{1}{y}$ (eller $y^{-1}$)
