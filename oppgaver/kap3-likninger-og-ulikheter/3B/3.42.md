Formler fra naturfag.

**a)** $E=mc^2$. Isoler $m$.

**b)** $F=\dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$. Isoler $r^2$, deretter $r$ (positiv).

**c)** $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{l}{g}}$. Isoler $l$.

**d)** $U=RI$. Isoler $R$, og finn $R$ når $U=12$ og $I=0{,}5$.

**e)** $\dfrac{1}{f}=\dfrac{1}{u}+\dfrac{1}{v}$. Isoler $f$.

## Hint

Kvadratrot fjernes ved å kvadrere. I e) fellesnevner $uv$, så inverter.

## Fasit

**a)** $m=\dfrac{E}{c^2}$. **Svar:** $m=\dfrac{E}{c^2}$

[STEG]

**b)** $r^2=\dfrac{GMm}{F}$, $r=\sqrt{\dfrac{GMm}{F}}$.
**Svar:** $r=\sqrt{\dfrac{GMm}{F}}$

[STEG]

**c)** $\dfrac{T}{2\pi}=\sqrt{\dfrac{l}{g}}$, $\dfrac{T^2}{4\pi^2}=\dfrac{l}{g}$, $l=\dfrac{gT^2}{4\pi^2}$.
**Svar:** $l=\dfrac{gT^2}{4\pi^2}$

[STEG]

**d)** $R=\dfrac{U}{I}=\dfrac{12}{0{,}5}=24$.
**Svar:** $R=24$

[STEG]

**e)** $\dfrac{1}{f}=\dfrac{v+u}{uv}$, $f=\dfrac{uv}{u+v}$.
**Svar:** $f=\dfrac{uv}{u+v}$
