Isoler den oppgitte bokstaven.

**a)** $A=l\cdot b$ for $l$

**b)** $C=2\pi r$ for $r$

**c)** $v=v_0+at$ for $t$

**d)** $y=mx+b$ for $x$

**e)** $P=2(l+b)$ for $b$

## Hint

Gjør det motsatte, i motsatt rekkefølge. $l$ ganges — da deler du.

## Fasit

**a)** $l=\dfrac{A}{b}$. **Svar:** $l=\dfrac{A}{b}$

[STEG]

**b)** $r=\dfrac{C}{2\pi}$. **Svar:** $r=\dfrac{C}{2\pi}$

[STEG]

**c)** $t=\dfrac{v-v_0}{a}$. **Svar:** $t=\dfrac{v-v_0}{a}$

[STEG]

**d)** $x=\dfrac{y-b}{m}$. **Svar:** $x=\dfrac{y-b}{m}$

[STEG]

**e)** $\dfrac{P}{2}=l+b$, $b=\dfrac{P}{2}-l$. **Svar:** $b=\dfrac{P}{2}-l$
