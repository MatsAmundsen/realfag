Løs med abc-formelen. Oppgi eksakt svar.

**a)** $x^2-6x+4=0$

**b)** $2x^2-5x-3=0$

## Hint

$x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$. Forenkle rota.

## Fasit

**a)** $a=1,b=-6,c=4$. $D=36-16=20=4\cdot 5$.
$x=\dfrac{6\pm 2\sqrt{5}}{2}=3\pm\sqrt{5}$.
**Svar:** $x=3+\sqrt{5}$ eller $x=3-\sqrt{5}$

[STEG]

**b)** $a=2,b=-5,c=-3$. $D=(-5)^2-4\cdot 2\cdot(-3)=25+24=49$.
$x=\dfrac{5\pm 7}{4}$. $x=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$ eller $x=\dfrac{-2}{4}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
**Svar:** $x=3$ eller $x=-\dfrac{1}{2}$
